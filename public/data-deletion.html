User Data Deletion Instructions

Hermes Instagram Assistant Meta Platform Compliance

Hermes Instagram Assistant values user data privacy. In compliance with Meta Platform rules, users can request deletion of their data at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion:

  1. Send an Instagram Direct Message to @itz_rishav_editz with the text "Delete my data" or "Clear history".
  2. Or email us at rishavkumarmgr@gmail.com with your Instagram username.

Confirmation of Deletion

Upon receiving your request, all transient chat context and associated user identifiers will be permanently removed from our serverless caches within 24 hours.
